# Passport bottle images

How bottles get their picture in Passport (kept in the Bible, sent to the app through the approved list):

1. **Official bottle photo** — from the producer, its parent company or its official importer, imported by link in the Bible (Passport settings → Photos). Shown on the app's dark tint; cut-out photos show without a box.
2. **Brand logo** — when no official bottle photo exists. Shown on a panel: cream (#F5F0E8) for dark lettering, dark olive (#2A3020) for light lettering (`photo_logo_light`). A real bottle photo always replaces a logo.
3. **Section icon** — when there is neither.

Logos never appear in collages or covers.
